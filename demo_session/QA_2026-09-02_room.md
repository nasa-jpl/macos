## The room's answers to the agent's questions

**Slide 9 — basins and merit scaling:**
1. Global search works *if the parameterization contains the path to
   the next valley* — canonical vehicle: **fictitious glasses
   (variable index and Abbe)** as a continuous relaxation.
2. Pressed on snap-back: **returning to real catalog glass does NOT
   revert the design — you have a new design form**; the arrival is
   permanent.  (Complements our measured sharpening: merely enrolling
   a DOF was not enough — the afocal solver never left the coaxial
   valley until *seeded* off-axis.)
3. Merit scaling: **normalization can work; all constraints are
   weighted.**  We shared our caveat: re-weighting silently unmade
   our constraint walls — re-check every wall after any re-weight, or
   enforce outside the score.

**Slide 15 — the afocal family (fully answered from the floor):**
1. **Price the cold-stop requirement at the pupil's EDGE only** —
   leakage is set by edge sharpness and edge wander; interior mapping
   distortion has no radiometric cost.
2. **Accept distortion for image quality — always a tradeoff** (the
   design's freedom is finite; mapping fidelity competes with
   sharpness).
3. The textbook frame: **balance pupil coma against image quality** —
   our 469→157 µm pupil blur at 39× image cost is that classical
   balance measured on a real family.
4. **Accept the distortion in the fore-optics; correct past the pupil
   in the instrument optics** — where the 30×-compressed beam makes
   corrective surfaces small and cheap.  Directly answers our
   compressed-beam-corrector question: standard division of labor.
