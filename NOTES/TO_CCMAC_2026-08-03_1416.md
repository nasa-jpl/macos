| supervisor | per-field EP reset |

Nothing anywhere resets per POKE for figure/rigid channels; the only
per-poke re-derivation in the stack is `FocalPlaneChannel`'s
`fp_mode` machinery (its own six columns, unwound with the poke).
`dw_dgrid_multi.m:169-188` states the intended semantics: *the FIELD
tilt is removed from the nominal, but a POKE's own tilt is retained.*

Physics (Dave's framing, agreed): with a frozen EP the gross field
tilt is common-mode between w_nom and every poked w at that field, so
it cancels exactly in the FD columns — which is why dwdx has always
looked tilt-clean.  What a per-field reset changes is only the
FIRST-order residual: d(frame term)/dx, the field-dependence of the
tilt's *sensitivity*.  Negligible at arcminute fields (why it never
surfaced), plausibly percent-level on tilt-coupled DOFs at wide
fields (0.5°-box, 30×-exit-angle class).  Consequence as shipped:
`run_sensitivities` joins a frozen-EP dwdx with per-field-reset
dwdz/dwdgrid in one model — nothing measured wrong at e2e/e5 fields,
but the mixed convention should not survive into wide-field use.
